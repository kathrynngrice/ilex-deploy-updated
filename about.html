About

Kathryn Noe Grice.

A leadership coach trained in adult development frameworks and research. Expert in helping people see themselves more clearly and explore their potential more courageously.

Kathryn works with adults in the middle of transitions — new roles, returning to work, pivoting without a career pathway, or building something of their own. Grounded, curious, and patient.
