About

Kathryn Noe Grice.

A leadership coach trained in adult-development research and the slow, careful work of helping people meet themselves more clearly.

Kathryn works with adults in the middle of real transitions — new roles, returning to work, leaving long careers, building something of their own. The orientation is grounded and curious. The pace is unhurried.

A note on approach

Growth happens in stages, not leaps. Most of the work is noticing what's already there.
