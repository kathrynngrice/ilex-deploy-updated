How the work moves

Floor, understory, canopy — small experiments build new habits without overwhelming the system.

01 — Floor

Notice what's already true.

We start with what's right in front of you. Noticing without judging or fixing.

02 — Understory

Try one small thing.

A single, specific experiment between sessions — something you can do during a busy week.

03 — Canopy

Sense the shift.

We return, name what got easier, and let the next step come into view. Growth happens in stages, not leaps.
