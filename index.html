How the work moves

Forest floor, understory, canopy — small experiments that build new habits without overwhelming the system.

01 — Forest Floor

Notice what's already true.

We start with what's right in front of you. No fixing. Just attention.

02 — Understory

Try something new - and small.

A single, specific experiment between sessions — designed by you, so you can actually do it during a busy week.

03 — Canopy

Sense what shifts.

You name what you noticed, and let the next step come into view. Growth happens in stages, not leaps.

Who this is for

Adults in the middle of a real change.

Between - not stuck. A new role, a new chapter, a quiet question that won't go away. You'll gradually notice new shifts and movement.
